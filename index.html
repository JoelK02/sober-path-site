Common questions

Why didn’t my count go up today?

A day counts once you check in and confirm it was alcohol-free. A day ends at 4 AM, so a late-night check-in still counts for that evening.

I forgot to check in for a few days.

Open the Calendar and tap any day to fill it in. Your streak comes back as soon as the gap is filled.

I had a drink. Have I lost everything?

No. A lapse or relapse resets the current streak, but your total days, savings and badges are never taken away.

How do I move to a new phone?

Sober Path has no account, so your data lives only on your phone. With Plus, go to You → Backup & restore, make an encrypted backup and save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone (Files, iCloud Drive, email). On the new phone, go through the short welcome, then restore it from the same screen; it replaces whatever is there. Restoring is always free. Keep your passphrase safe: without it the backup can’t be opened, even by us.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Sober Path and choose a size. You → Widget in the app has the details.

How do I restore Plus?

Open the Plus screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel Plus?

In iOS Settings, tap your name → Subscriptions → Sober Path Plus → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least a day before it renews. Anything you made with Plus (backups, entries, garden items already grown) stays yours.

Is my journal private?

Yes. It is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy.
