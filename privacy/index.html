Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Sober Path keeps everything you write on your phone. There is no account, no server holding your data, no advertising and no tracking.

In short

  • Your days, moods, notes, journal and cravings are stored only on your phone.
  • We never see them, and nobody else does unless you share them yourself.
  • If you buy Plus, Apple and our payment service RevenueCat handle the purchase. They see an anonymous ID and the receipt, never what you have written.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data.

Who we are

Sober Path is made by Joel Xun, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at joelkwoh002@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is kept in the app’s own storage on your device:

  • your day log: which days were alcohol-free, lapses and relapses, and the date you started;
  • check-ins: mood, triggers and private notes;
  • journal entries;
  • urge-surfing sessions and how intense the urges were;
  • your usual weekly spend, used to work out what you have saved;
  • settings such as appearance, theme, reminder time and widget wording.

All counts, savings, milestones and insights are worked out on your phone from that log. None of it is sent to us or anyone else. iOS may include the app’s data in your own iCloud or computer backup of the phone, under Apple’s terms, if you have those turned on.

Location (optional)

The garden’s sky follows the real sun and moon. By default it uses the main city of your time zone, which needs no permission. If you choose to under You → Sun & moon, the app asks iOS for your location once, rounds it to about 11 km (0.1°) and keeps only that rounded position on your phone. It is never sent anywhere and is not included in backups. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone. It never mentions alcohol and goes through no server.

Widgets

Widgets show your count and garden. The app passes them that information on your phone only. They are discreet by default and only say “alcohol-free” if you switch that on.

Purchases (Sober Path Plus)

Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

To check whether you have Plus, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat starts only when you open the Plus screen, or when the app opens if you have bought or restored Plus before. It receives:

  • a random, anonymous ID made by the app, not linked to your name or Apple ID;
  • the App Store receipt and the purchases on it;
  • basic technical details such as the app version, iOS version, device model, country and IP address, as with any internet request.

RevenueCat never receives your day log, notes, journal or anything else you write. We use this information only to unlock Plus, restore it on your devices, prevent fraud and see overall sales figures, never for advertising or tracking.

Backups, exports and reports

These files are made on your phone and go only where you send them through the share sheet.

  • Encrypted backup (Plus): encrypted with AES-256 using a passphrase only you know. We cannot open it, and if the passphrase is lost the backup cannot be recovered.
  • CSV export and PDF progress report: not encrypted, so anyone you share them with can read them. The report leaves out journal notes unless you switch them on.

Links to other sites

The app links to findahelpline.com to help you find support, and to Apple pages for subscriptions and terms. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No connection to Apple Health.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting Sober Path removes all of it from the device; backups and exports you have saved elsewhere stay where you put them. The anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat is kept while it is needed to restore Plus. To have it deleted, email us with the date you bought Plus and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, Malaysia’s PDPA or Californian law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because the data you write never leaves your phone, you already control it fully. For anything held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Children

Sober Path is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will say so in the app before it applies.

Not medical advice

Sober Path is a self-help tool, not a medical service. If stopping drinking makes you unwell, or you drink heavily every day, talk to a doctor: alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous.
